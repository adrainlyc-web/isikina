How to Withdraw, Step by Step

  1. Check your turnover. Make sure you've wagered at least 2x your deposit (see below).

  3. Message Isikina on WhatsApp. Use the official Chat on WhatsApp button and say you want to withdraw. Include your 918Kaya game ID and the amount.
  4. Confirm your bank details in the chat. Isikina confirms where the money is going.
  5. Receive your payment. It's sent by bank transfer, usually within 24 hours.

Close the 918Kaya game before you ask, so your balance is up to date.

The 2x Turnover Rule

A normal deposit needs 2x turnover before it can be withdrawn. Turnover means the total amount you've wagered, not what you've won. Deposit K100, and you need to wager K200 in total.

Wagering counts every bet, win or lose, so winning a round doesn't use it up and losing a round doesn't reset it.

Minimum Withdrawal

Your minimum withdrawal is twice your deposit, and never less than K50. A few examples:

  • Deposit K10 to K24 → minimum withdrawal K50
  • Deposit K50 → minimum withdrawal K100
  • Deposit K100 → minimum withdrawal K200

K10 is the smallest deposit, so K50 is the smallest withdrawal anyone can make. Withdrawals are for players who have deposited.

If a Withdrawal Is Delayed

  • Check the turnover and minimum first. Those are the most common reasons a request waits.
  • Keep the chat open and reply quickly if Isikina asks you to confirm something.
  • Allow for bank processing time before asking again.
  • Keep your screenshots and receipts until the money arrives.
  • Only use the official WhatsApp number. Never pay a "fee" to release a withdrawal, since a genuine withdrawal doesn't need one.
